# Book context

## The book

- **Title:** *The Alignment-Industrial Complex*
- **Subtitle:** *How Fragmented Authority Destroys a Company's Ability to Compete*
- **Author:** Adrian McPhee
- **Publisher:** Simple is Advanced
- **Book edition:** v2.0.3 review, 5 September 2026
- **Field Guide protocol:** v0.3
- **Public Field Guide:** <https://github.com/adrianmcphee/the_alignment_industrial_complex>
- **Book information:** <https://simpleisadvanced.com/>

The book's publication remains separate from this public guide. The review
edition number does not change the guide's schema or rule-set versions.

The book argues that permanent alignment work is often a compensating industry
for fragmented authority. Strategy, process definitions, architecture, code,
and production evidence can remain locally correct while describing different
companies. AI amplifies the result. A company with coherent authority can
amplify good decisions. A company dependent on recurring alignment meetings
can amplify the circus until it breaks.

The operating response is to draw boundaries around complete processes, give
one owner the right to change the ordinary process, place explicit contracts at
real seams, preserve genuinely independent rights, and make consequential
claims testable against production.

## Why this guide exists

The book closes with a short practical guide to starting on Monday. The full
implementation protocol is maintained separately here, including the
machine-readable forms of five worked artefacts:

1. process definition;
2. model evaluation record;
3. published contract;
4. unit charter;
5. drift report.

The guide also supplies schemas, evidence joins, validation rules, change
mechanics, minimum examples, complete examples, and deliberately broken
fixtures. It remains a small living protocol. Reader tooling, telemetry-product
patterns, and migration mechanics can be added only when a later version earns
them.

## Repository boundary

This public repository is the distribution home of the Technical Field Guide.
The complete book source remains in a separate private book repository. The
guide's source package is maintained with the book's build inputs and exported
here with its validation record, PDF and archive. Only the guide and the
book's front and back cover images are included in this public repository;
the manuscript, editorial plans, other production assets and publishing system
remain outside it.

The book's closing Monday guide points readers here for the complete protocol
and companion artefacts.

Future changes should preserve that separation. A guide release must update
the change log, validation record, PDF, source archive, and checksums together.

The stable reading link is [technical-field-guide.pdf](technical-field-guide.pdf),
and the matching download is [technical-field-guide-source.zip](technical-field-guide-source.zip).
Versioned copies and checksums are in [releases/v0.3](releases/v0.3/).
